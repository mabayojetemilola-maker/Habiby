The beginning

When eyes first met

He was your mother’s student. You only came to greet her at school — a simple visit, a simple salaam. But Allah writes stories in the smallest moments.

He saw you. And something quiet and real began to grow in his heart — not rushed, not careless, but sincere.

The confession

Big feelings, honest words

He found the courage to speak. Not with pride — with humility.

“Dearest Basheerah, I have gotten this kind of big feelings for you. The feeling is love, and it’s nothing if not reciprocal. I hereby seek and plead to know your interest in me.”

He feared spoiling the trust your mother had in him. He feared being an idiot in front of those who were proud of him. Still, he chose honesty — because real love does not hide forever.
